/ Case study · Meta Ads

3,382 messaging conversations at ₹5.49 each.

Challenge

Bring in a steady flow of enquiries by message at a low cost per conversation.

Approach

  • Ran 14 campaigns, mainly with the messaging objective so people could start a chat straight from the ad
  • Tested engagement, video and Reel-view campaigns alongside them
  • Tracked cost per conversation for every campaign in Ads Manager

Tools used: Instagram · Facebook · Meta Ads Manager · ChatGPT · Photoshop
