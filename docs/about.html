About Me

OG

Hi, I’m Om, people call me OMG.

I’m an SEO & Content Strategist based in Delhi, currently at AdLift Marketing. Over the last three-plus years I’ve worked across agencies and in-house teams on technical SEO, content strategy, local search and paid social.

Today, a lot of my work sits where classic search meets AI search. I track how brands get cited across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, then shape content so it earns those citations as well as rankings.

I hold a BCA from Indira Gandhi National Open University and work in English and Hindi.

“Good SEO is clear answers, built on a technically sound site, measured honestly. Rankings and citations follow from that.”
